Senior NLP Engineer

About the role
We are building language understanding features for a document intelligence product. You will design, fine-tune and ship transformer-based models that extract, classify and summarize text at scale.

Requirements:
- 4+ years of experience in machine learning or NLP
- Strong Python and PyTorch skills
- Hands-on experience with Hugging Face Transformers and fine-tuning BERT-style models
- Experience with named entity recognition and text classification
- Experience building and deploying REST APIs (FastAPI or Flask)
- Solid understanding of model evaluation and metrics
- Master's degree in Computer Science, Linguistics or a related field

Nice to have:
- Experience with LLMs, RAG pipelines and vector databases
- Docker and cloud deployment (AWS or GCP)
- Publications at ACL, EMNLP or similar venues
- spaCy and semantic search experience
